Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Resource Vault handles information when you visit and use this website.

1. Introduction

Resource Vault is an independent online directory that helps visitors discover websites and digital resources.

This Privacy Policy explains the general approach to information and privacy on the Resource Vault website.

2. Information We Collect

Resource Vault does not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information simply to browse the resource directory.

If you voluntarily contact Resource Vault by email, the information you include in your message may be received and used only as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, investigate an issue, or communicate with you.

Do not send passwords, payment information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information through the contact address.

3. Website Usage Information

Like many websites, technical information may be processed by the hosting provider or other infrastructure used to deliver the website. This may include information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and basic technical logs.

Resource Vault does not use this information to personally identify visitors unless required for a legitimate technical, security, or legal purpose.

4. Cookies

Resource Vault may use cookies or similar technologies if they are introduced through website functionality, analytics, advertising, or other services.

Cookies may be used to support website functionality, understand general website usage, measure performance, or provide advertising services where applicable.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features or third-party services may not function as intended when cookies are disabled.

5. Third-Party Websites

Resource Vault contains links to third-party websites and services. When you select an external link, you leave Resource Vault and are subject to the privacy practices and terms of the third-party website.

Resource Vault does not control how third-party websites collect, use, store, or process information.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website before providing personal information.

6. Advertising

Resource Vault may display advertising in the future, including advertising provided through third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense, if the website is approved and configured to use those services.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, personalize, measure, or limit advertisements in accordance with their own policies and applicable requirements.

This section will be updated if advertising services are enabled on the website.

7. Analytics

Resource Vault may use analytics services in the future to understand general website traffic, page performance, and how visitors interact with the website.

If an analytics service is added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to identify the relevant service and explain its applicable data practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Resource Vault is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Resource Vault, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

9. Data Security

Reasonable measures may be used to help protect information associated with the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Resource Vault introduces new features, services, analytics, advertising, or other changes that affect privacy practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or a privacy-related concern, please contact Resource Vault through the Contact page.
